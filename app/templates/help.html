What this app is not

  • A prototype. It was built for a competition and for demonstration. It is not a finished service.
  • Not able to judge how serious something is. It cannot tell a small problem from a dangerous one, and it never tries to.
  • Not a monitoring service. Nobody is watching these records. No one is alerted if a number changes, and no one will contact you.
  • Not a replacement for a doctor. It does not diagnose, does not change a prescription, and does not tell you what to take.
  • Not connected to anyone. It has no link to a hospital, a clinic, an ambulance service, or a family member.
