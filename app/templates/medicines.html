My medicines

This page explains what a medicine is for

It does not tell you how much to take. It does not tell you what to do about a missed dose. It does not tell you to change, stop or start anything. Those questions belong with your doctor or your pharmacist, and asking them is the right thing to do, not a nuisance.

Every explanation below comes from a written source that is named next to it. If there is no explanation for a medicine, this page says so instead of guessing.
