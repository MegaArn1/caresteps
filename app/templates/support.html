What has been difficult

Recording a blood pressure reading is easy on a good day. It is the other days that matter, and they are usually about something else: the day was busy, the cuff was hard to use, the reason for the medicine was not clear, or something felt wrong.

So when there is no record, this app asks what got in the way. A missing record is a question, not a conclusion. It is not a score, and it is not something you got wrong.

Every entry below is kept in the words you used. Where the app has added a suggestion of its own, it is marked as a suggestion and not as your words.
